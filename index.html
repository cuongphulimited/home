FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often. Can't find yours? Just ask us.

How much does a software project cost?

It depends on scope, complexity and timeline. After a free consultation we send a detailed estimate with clear milestones, offering fixed-price or time-and-materials options.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

A corporate website typically takes 2–4 weeks. A mobile app or custom business system usually takes 2–6 months, depending on features and integrations.

Will I own the source code?

Yes. On project completion and payment, you receive full ownership of the source code, documentation and related assets.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support packages covering bug fixes, updates, monitoring and new feature development.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide and communicate in both Vietnamese and English.
